## Current default mode

Choose **Build my base** for autonomous play. Start with any affordable building, tap it for production, collect finished batches and choose supply requests from **Orders and supplies**. Expand using earned credits. You can mine while production runs, move buildings and choose multiple factories. There are no mandatory quiz interruptions in this mode. Choose **Guided missions** only if you want the older step-by-step campaign described below. The two modes save separately.

# Playing OxyNest Moon Base

Open PLAY-OXYNEST.bat from the outer project folder, or open kids/index.html directly. Choose Explorer first. A space nickname is optional; do not enter a real name. EN / 中文 changes language. The speaker button controls short effects; there is no music.

Follow the large prompt and arrow. One target is highlighted. The question mark repeats the hint; after eight idle seconds the mascot points again. During a question, the pointer identifies the question rather than revealing its answer.

## First mission

1. Choose Go outside. Wait briefly for the astronaut to reach the rocks, then tap the highlighted spot.
2. When dust slows work, answer the dust question and clear it. Read the short learning card.
3. Return when prompted. Suit-air exhaustion returns the astronaut with the material and does not fail the mission.
4. Tap the highlighted empty slot and build a battery. Its payment and result are saved once; a short countdown presents the construction.
5. Handle lunar night by choosing how to power the base without sunlight. Read what happened, why and the everyday comparison.
6. The summary shows learned cards, stars and a Moon badge. Continue to the next mission.

## Later missions and recovery

Ice mining unlocks after the first mission. A Processor turns rock into air or ice into water using game power. Later tasks introduce a greenhouse, radiation shelter, oxygen maker and a final building choice. Four missions lead into free building and upgrades up to level three. The Knowledge book holds learned cards.

Wrong answers lower survival and show an explanation. Try again offers another choice; a correct answer after an error earns a smaller fixed reward. At zero survival, MISSION FAILED offers Try again or Quit. Mission retry restores reserves and keeps current buildings, rocks, ice, stars and cards. Quit returns to the menu without deleting the base.

Explorer has two answers, no event timer and icon-based counts. Engineer adds simple numbers and a pausable event timer. Commander adds units, a shorter timer and chained events. These counts and meters are game rules, not certified mission measurements.

Quit at the upper right returns to the start screen. Continue resumes a saved level. Starting over requires confirmation and resets that level. Close the browser tab after returning to the menu. Saves are local and are not automatically transferred between computers, browsers or ports.

Learn more appears only for a permitted NASA link. Opening it leaves the offline game for an external page; ask a grown-up first. A missing source shows a placeholder. The supplied source statements still require team review.
