# NASA Data and Resources

OxyNest uses NASA-referenced educational text to connect Moon base activities with lunar conditions, resource use and life-support ideas. Current learning text and source fields are embedded in game/js/i18n.js and game/js/content.js. The game does not fetch a live NASA dataset or load the earlier nine-file research pack at runtime.

## Reality and game design

The learning topics include energy storage, local resource use, oxygen generation, water recovery, space gardening and environmental hazards. Building prices, power labels, material conversions, survival meters, rewards and processing durations are simplified game-design assumptions. They must not be presented as verified NASA equipment specifications or as a predictive mission model.

Fourteen cards have supplied citations and two (Moon dust and Space rocks) retain fact placeholders. The game hides Learn more when a card has no source. Source wording and exact numerical claims still require content review. In particular, the NTRS record attached to Clean air has not been established as support for its CDRA wording. A valid NASA URL alone does not establish that a claim is correct.

This prototype does not claim source-content verification, NASA approval or award eligibility. OxyNest is an independent student project and is not endorsed by NASA. Other resources and AI assistance are disclosed in CREDITS.md and project-metadata.json.
